## Worth studying

- [TCBP-JOBS: GPU-Accelerated Quantum Error Correction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21321) — Howe, Maurya, Olle, Basov, Li, Lietz, Young, Svore: a learned fault-probability model guides parallel GPU beam search, matching the Tesseract decoder's accuracy at serial speedups up to 13,000x.
- [QCap: a physics-corrected graph neural network for the capacitance matrix of any planar superconducting device layout](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22057) — Shanto, Das, Levenson-Falk: predicts a superconducting device's full capacitance matrix to sub-percent error in seconds-to-minutes instead of hours-to-intractable simulation.
- [AI Scientist Discovered Robust Quantum Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21765) — Yan et al.: a multi-agent AI framework (QORA) discovers a novel control pulse sequence on real NV-diamond centers, nearly doubling measured coherence time.
- [Real-time noise adaptive quantum error correction by multi-agent learning](https://doi.org/10.1088/2058-9565/aebdc4) — Guatto, Preti, Schilling, Calarco, Cárdenas-López, Motzoi: offline multi-agent RL discovers QEC codes/cycles; an online layer adapts to error-channel drift without retraining.
- [How many photons can one GPU keep up with?](https://www.quandela.com/resources/blog/photonic-quantum-simulation-benchmark-nvidia-h100/) — Quandela: a measured, open-sourced "simulability frontier" for classical GPU simulation of photonic sampling, a reusable yardstick for photonic quantum-advantage claims.
- [Agentic AI for Discovering Fault-Tolerant Quantum Circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18504) — Lahiri, Marquardt: an agentic-AI framework designing circuits across the full QEC stack (logical-state prep, logical gates, logical routines), not just one hand-picked circuit type.
- [Tessa: Encoding Classical Data Across an Entire Quantum Processor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18440) — a hardware-aware, device-wide data-encoding scheme experimentally packing up to 1,568 real values onto IBM's Phoenix and Fez processors.
- [Multi-head Quantum Neural Networks: Replacing Transformer Blocks with NISQ Modules inside a 30-Billion-Parameter LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15250) — Amoussou, Aizpurua, Singh, Orús, Jacobsen: the first reported swap of a quantum module for transformer blocks inside an already-pretrained 30B-parameter LLM, not a from-scratch toy model.
- [An unconditional distribution learning advantage with shallow quantum circuits](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-026-01370-4) — Pirnay, Jerbi, Seifert, Eisert (npj QI, Nov 10): an unconditional PAC distribution-learning separation for constant-depth quantum circuits, uplifting a known shallow-circuit sampling advantage into a learning-theory result.
- [Simulating Gaussian boson sampling on graphs in polynomial time](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-11-10-2268) — Anand, Chen, Cryan, Freifeld, Goldberg, Guo, Zhang (Quantum, Nov 10): the distribution most graph-theoretic/molecular-similarity GBS applications sample from admits a polynomial-time classical sampler.
- [A Hardware-Trained Generative Quantum Eigensolver for Sample-Based Quantum Diagonalization of Organotin Hydroxides up to 70 Qubits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13755) — Kumar, Keithley, Iwamori, Kanno: trains a transformer circuit-generating policy with a real IBM Heron processor inside the training loop at 46/70 qubits — the first ML-assisted SQD variant.
- [Machine-learning decoders for fusion-based quantum computing in real time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13630) — Barter, Bridgeman, Dawson, Farrelly: PRISM, a transformer decoder exploiting known erasure locations in photonic fusion-based fault-tolerant protocols — the first ML-decoder result on a non-superconducting platform.
