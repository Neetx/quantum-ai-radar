## Worth studying

- [An unconditional distribution learning advantage with shallow quantum circuits](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-026-01370-4) — Pirnay, Jerbi, Seifert, Eisert (npj QI, Nov 10): an unconditional PAC distribution-learning separation for constant-depth quantum circuits, uplifting a known shallow-circuit sampling advantage into a learning-theory result.
- [Simulating Gaussian boson sampling on graphs in polynomial time](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2026-11-10-2268) — Anand, Chen, Cryan, Freifeld, Goldberg, Guo, Zhang (Quantum, Nov 10): the distribution most graph-theoretic/molecular-similarity GBS applications sample from admits a polynomial-time classical sampler.
- [A Hardware-Trained Generative Quantum Eigensolver for Sample-Based Quantum Diagonalization of Organotin Hydroxides up to 70 Qubits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13755) — Kumar, Keithley, Iwamori, Kanno: trains a transformer circuit-generating policy with a real IBM Heron processor inside the training loop at 46/70 qubits — the first ML-assisted SQD variant.
- [Machine-learning decoders for fusion-based quantum computing in real time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13630) — Barter, Bridgeman, Dawson, Farrelly: PRISM, a transformer decoder exploiting known erasure locations in photonic fusion-based fault-tolerant protocols — the first ML-decoder result on a non-superconducting platform.
- [Pasqal Demonstrates Logical Qubits Outperform Physical Qubits Solving Differential Equations](https://www.pasqal.com/newsroom/pasqal-demonstrates-logical-qubits-outperform-physical-qubits-solving-differential-equations-an-industry-first-for-neutral-atom-quantum-computing) — Pasqal (Mathiot, Garnaoui, Leriche, Philip, Henriet et al.; May 20): a quantum-kernel differential-equation solver run at both the physical and logical level, with the logical implementation outperforming its physical counterpart end-to-end.
- [A proof of the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture](https://psi.inc/research/aaronson-ambainis/) — Physical Superintelligence (Turturean, Halverson et al.; Nov 6): an AI research system found and Lean-verified a proof of the 17-year-old conjecture, independently confirmed by outside experts and Scott Aaronson himself.
- [The Compendium of Super-Quadratic Computational Quantum Advantages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32595) — Kammerlander, O'Brien, King, Somma, Babbush et al. (Oct 23): a multi-lab consensus map of 24 problems with credible super-quadratic quantum advantage, scoped to the real-world constraints needed to realize it.
- [Quantum Knowledge Distillation: State-of-the-art, Applications and Open Problems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08461) — Al-Sulaiti, Chen, Dastagir, Farouk, Al-Kuwari (Nov 5): a comprehensive survey of knowledge distillation in quantum machine learning.
- [Quantum-Classical Auxiliary-Field Quantum Monte Carlo at the Edge of Practicability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19239) — IQM + Volkswagen Group Innovation (hardware-validated Nov 5, orig. Jun 2026): up to a 124x classical-runtime reduction for QC-AFQMC, validated on a 54-qubit superconducting processor.
- [Reimagining computational quantum research with AI scientists](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02833) — Du, Zhao, Wang, Lu, Zhang, Tao (Nov 2): introduces OmniQ, benchmarking AI scientists on reproducing 59 published computational-quantum-science papers.
- [GFlowQEC: Generative Flow Networks that Learn to Sample Quantum LDPC Codes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06819) — Batista, Falcao, Machado (Nov 4): the first generative flow network for quantum code design.
- [FirstQFM Demonstrates Quantum Reservoir Computing on Rigetti's 108-Qubit Processor](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/firstqfm-demonstrates-quantum-reservoir-computing-on-rigettis-108-qubit-processor-to-outperform-classical-financial-benchmarks/) — FirstQFM + SW7 Group + Rigetti (Nov 3): a blinded out-of-sample financial-forecasting pilot beats classical ML baselines while explicitly disclaiming general quantum advantage.
